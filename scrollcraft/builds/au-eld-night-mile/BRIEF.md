# AU ELD Service · Night Mile

Interview source: the client’s direct feedback across this task. This redesign reuses those answers because the client explicitly said the desired direction is already understood.

## Eight interview answers

1. Vibe and references

   > “i like night dispatch one.”
   >
   > “premium qilib qil”
   >
   > “do not make it look like ai generated website.”

   Night Dispatch, premium, focused and human-directed. The supplied AU ELD logo is the primary reference.

2. Scroll journey

   > “yaxshi pastga tushadigon, animationli, website bo'lishi kere.”
   >
   > “when i scroll there should be animations in each scroll.”

   One continuous downward journey: brand recognition, services on the road, a memorable route transformation, then direct contact.

3. Energy curve

   > “less distractions”
   >
   > “make something awesome”

   Calm at entry, steadily moving through the middle, quiet immediately before one strong visual peak, then simple and useful at the close.

4. Stage feelings and remembered moment

   The established direction is confidence, momentum, control and readiness. The remembered moment is the blue slash in the AU logo becoming the illuminated highway carrying the truck.

5. One thing it should do that other sites do not

   > “it should be more visible and it should not be just line.”

   The logo’s blue slash becomes a broad reflective road, drives the whole journey, then divides into three usable contact exits.

6. Distance from premium-minimal

   > “juda ko'p text keremas.”
   >
   > “make it best website with less distractions.”

   Premium-minimal in copy and interface. Expressive only in the road transformation and scroll motion.

7. One unbroken world or distinct scenes

   > “some pages are with colors in the background or with image and some pages are just empty in the background. and i don't like it.”

   One unbroken nocturnal highway world. No section seams, empty backgrounds or unrelated colour changes.

8. Existing assets

   > “this is a logo of our logistic service”

   Supplied AU ELD logo, derived brand mark and existing night-dispatch truck photograph. No new generated imagery is required.

## Business brief

- Audience: trucking operators and drivers who need ELD setup, compliance support or fast driver help.
- Belief: AU ELD Service keeps the operation moving when ELD work gets complicated.
- One action label: **Contact**.
- Art direction: nocturne highway photography, wet asphalt reflections and one brand-cobalt route.

## Feeling curve

1. **Confidence** · The full AU mark owns the first screen with one short promise.
2. **Momentum** · The blue route grows into a road while three concise service waypoints pass.
3. **Awe** · After a quiet interval, the road expands beneath the truck and the logo slash becomes the entire route.
4. **Readiness** · The road settles, reunites with the AU mark and opens three direct contact exits.

## Peak

Peak stage: 3, Awe.

Visitor sentence: “The blue slash left the logo and became the highway under the truck.”

## Tell-someone sentence

It’s the site where the AU logo opens into a night highway, carries the truck forward, then becomes the contact route.

## Authored silence

Immediately before the road reaches full width, service copy clears, route particles slow and the truck world holds for a short interval. The road itself continues drawing, so this is anticipation rather than dead scroll.

## Copy budget and honesty

- Visible marketing copy stays under 100 words.
- No invented statistics, response times or compliance claims.
- Phone, email and Telegram remain centralized sample values until the client supplies final contact details.
