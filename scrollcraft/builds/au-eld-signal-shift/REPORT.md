# AU ELD Service · Signal Shift report

## Outcome

A premium one-page AU ELD homepage built as an operational signal ledger. The page opens with the real AU mark, proves the support workflow, pans through every included service, lands the free-week offer and finishes with direct Phone, Telegram and Email actions.

The design takes high-level lessons from Vectus — dark editorial restraint, strong type hierarchy, a persistent atmospheric field and purposeful motion — without copying its assets, identity, copy or exact section composition.

## Grammar decision

Chosen grammar: **Operational signal ledger**.

The unit is a support signal rather than a generic feature card. Five distinct scenes share one persistent support field. The support sequence is the peak and the only long pin; service breadth moves once as a lateral manifest; the close turns the completed trace into direct contact routes.

The eight stock grammars lost because they either repeated an earlier AU ELD build, required a photographic world or fake product dashboard, or could not carry both the requested swipe and the human-led support proof without adding unnecessary copy.

## Signature move

**Shift Trace.** A route grows from the AU mark, stamps the support checkpoints and then fans into the three real contact channels. The canvas pulses, background depth and trace all react to scroll as one system rather than as unrelated decoration.

Tell-someone sentence: “Every support check sent a pulse through the AU mark, and the finished trace became the contact channels.”

## Journey and feeling curve

1. **Recognition · Curiosity** — “A second set of eyes on every shift” assembles around the live AU mark.
2. **Support proof · Relief** — 3–4 checks per shift, log errors, ELD status and the 20-mile readiness prompt advance under scroll.
3. **Service range · Control** — the included service groups move laterally as one manifest.
4. **Offer · Confidence** — one week free, $120 per truck/month, no hidden fees and the service promise land together.
5. **Contact · Resolve** — Phone leads, with Telegram and Email available immediately beside it.

- Client-supplied AU ELD logo, preserved unchanged inside an intentional rounded dispatch-monitor frame.

- A transparent logo extraction candidate was generated during exploration but rejected because preserving the exact client mark mattered more; it is not referenced by the project.
