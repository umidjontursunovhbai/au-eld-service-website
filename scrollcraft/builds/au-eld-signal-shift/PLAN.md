# AU ELD Signal Shift: Build Plan

## Page grammar

**Operational signal ledger.** A named grammar specific to AU ELD.

The page is one shift record accumulating under the visitor's hand. The persistent ambient field is not a decorative network or a fake dashboard. It is the ledger: every act adds a visible support stamp, and the completed trace becomes the contact handoff.

### Grammar rules

- The unit is a support signal, not a generic section or a card.
- Five distinct scenes share one persistent code-native telemetry field.
- Chrome is a slim vertical coverage spine with clickable checkpoints. The fixed top element carries brand and one contact action only.
- The hero is an off-axis editorial title orbiting the real AU mark.
- The support scene is the peak and the only long pin.
- Service breadth moves laterally once, as a manifest rather than a grid.
- The close converts the completed trace into a direct phone-first switchboard.
- No photographic ground, video scrub, app dashboard, route worldflight, equal feature-card grid, section counters, or random tech lines.

## Why the eight stock grammars lost

- **Filmic one-shot:** the brief asks for distinct scenes and fast native scroll, not one carried film.
- **Chaptered editorial:** the persistent live support trace is more important than printed folios and hard chapter plates.
- **Live surface:** AU ELD is a service, and inventing a product dashboard would violate the honesty rule.
- **Continuous world:** there is no single geographic journey, and the previous build already claimed it.
- **Typographic poster:** it forbids the requested swipe and cannot carry the support sequence clearly enough.
- **Gallery / catalog:** the page needs one argument about coverage, not an options-first collection.
- **Split stage:** no two-sided comparison exists for the full page.
- **Rhythmic cutlist:** constant high energy conflicts with “less distractions” and the quiet premium reference.

## Signature move

**Shift Trace.** A bespoke SVG/canvas trace grows from the AU mark as the page moves. Each support checkpoint sends one pulse, stamps a label into the trace, and remains recorded. At the end, that same trace fans into Phone, Telegram, and Email. Scroll position drives the actual painted trace and the verification state; the engine remains unchanged.

Tell-someone test: “Every support check sent a pulse through the AU logo, and the finished trace became the contact channels.”

## Responsive rules

- Desktop: asymmetric hero, right-edge coverage spine, pinned support sequence, full-width lateral manifest, phone-first contact switchboard.
- Tablet: no cropped masks; support copy and signal core share a 44/56 split; rail keeps at least 60vw overflow.
- Mobile: compact logo + phone header, no squeezed menu, native swipe fallback for the manifest, all text remains within 16px gutters, contact order Phone → Telegram → Email.
- Short landscape: hero may grow beyond one viewport; no fixed-height clipping; support details use a compact row layout.
- Reduced motion: signal stamps remain readable, canvas becomes static, rail becomes native horizontal snap, and all content remains reachable.
