## Business brief

- What it is and who it is for: AU ELD Service is a logistics support service for trucking operators and drivers who need ELD setup, compliance guidance, and responsive driver help.
- What the visitor must believe: AU ELD Service turns a confusing ELD issue into one clear next action.
- One next action and one label: **Contact dispatch**.
- Assets: supplied AU ELD logo and mark.
- Art direction: Night Dispatch interpreted as a cold, tactile operations console. Real semantic UI and code-native route graphics, not generated dashboard imagery.

## Feeling curve

1. **Alert** · A live sample night shift is already running and a driver request arrives without a marketing intro.
2. **Concern** · The request exposes gaps across device, driver, and record data as the route surface becomes unsettled.
3. **Focus** · The surface isolates what matters and groups the available service paths into clear operational choices.
4. **Control** · The selected help path steadies the route and visibly advances the incident toward resolution.
5. **Relief** · After a deliberate quiet beat, fractured log sheets snap into one verified record. This is the largest and most memorable stage.
6. **Readiness** · The verified record folds into an actual contact composer with direct phone, email, and Telegram routes.

## Peak

Peak stage: 5, Relief.

Visitor sentence: “The log sheets broke apart, snapped into one clean record, then folded into the message field.”

## Tell-someone sentence

It’s the site where a live night-shift incident breaks into fragments, resolves into one clean record, and becomes the message you send to dispatch.

## Authored silence

Immediately before the record snap, the route motion settles, status chatter disappears, and only one restrained scan pulse remains for a short scroll interval. This is intentional anticipation, not dead scroll; the pulse and scan phase must still update the verification state.

## Content honesty

- The operational incident is visibly labelled **Sample night shift**.
- All incident values are local sample data used to demonstrate the interaction, not claims about AU ELD Service performance.
- Contact details remain centralized placeholders until the client supplies final phone, email, and Telegram values.
