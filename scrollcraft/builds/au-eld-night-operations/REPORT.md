# AU ELD Service · Night Operations report

## Outcome

Built and locally delivered a new main page using Scrollcraft’s **Live surface** grammar. The page behaves like a working sample night-operations console instead of a conventional marketing landing page.

## Why this grammar

The client requested Night Dispatch, fewer distractions, animation on every scroll, a more visible moving background, varied swipes, and easy contact. A persistent live surface makes all of those requests one coherent interaction instead of a stack of unrelated sections.

## Signature move

Three sample event sources separate from the route, align by timestamp, and snap into one verified incident. The verified record remains on the map while its incident context is handed to the contact composer.

## Assets and cost

- Used the client-supplied AU ELD logo and transparent mark.
- Created the route world, district fields, scan surface, and record animation in HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript.
- No generated imagery, video, or paid generation credits were used.
